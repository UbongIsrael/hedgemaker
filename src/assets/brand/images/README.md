# Frontend editorial images

Original HedgeMaker artwork generated on 6 October 2026. Full-resolution 1536 × 1024 PNGs, generated-file identifiers, and SHA-256 hashes are preserved in [the redesign archive](../../../../docs/redesign/images/manifest.json).

- `currency-sculpture.webp`: forest-green folded banknote-inspired paper with original fine guilloché and woven diamond motifs, a lime paper diamond, and a blue glass sphere on ivory. No literal currency, numbers, or copied portrait. Hero, risk card, learning, and participation illustration.
- `importer.webp`: an illustrative Nigerian entrepreneur reviewing inventory in a warm clay-toned workspace, with boxes and a woven workbench textile. Buyer image card.
- `dollar-earner.webp`: an illustrative Nigerian creative professional at a sunlit desk, with a blue notebook and a woven diamond wall panel. Seller image card.

The people are generated illustrations of user situations, not real customers or endorsements. The three whole originals are encoded as WebP quality 88 for delivery; card cropping is handled by CSS. Keep originals and delivery files separate when revising the artwork. Fonts and logos are separate assets, never baked into these photographs.
