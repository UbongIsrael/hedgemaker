# Frontend price illustrations and archived concepts

Original HedgeMaker artwork generated on 6 October 2026. Full-resolution 1536 × 1024 PNGs, generated-file identifiers, and SHA-256 hashes are preserved in [the redesign archive](../../../../docs/redesign/images/manifest.json).

Current artwork:

- `buyer-price-phone.webp`: a rising blue price chart on a clean device, with fine engraved chart fill and a patterned paper support. Buyer card and learning hero.
- `seller-price-phone.webp`: a falling green price chart with woven/guilloché fill, a blue reference line, and restrained lime detail. Seller card.

Archived initial concepts, retained for future reference:

- `currency-sculpture.webp`: forest-green folded banknote-inspired paper with original fine guilloché and woven diamond motifs, a lime paper diamond, and a blue glass sphere on ivory. No literal currency, numbers, or copied portrait. Previously used in the hero, risk card, learning, and participation illustration.
- `importer.webp`: an illustrative Nigerian entrepreneur reviewing inventory in a warm clay-toned workspace, with boxes and a woven workbench textile. Previous buyer image card.
- `dollar-earner.webp`: an illustrative Nigerian creative professional at a sunlit desk, with a blue notebook and a woven diamond wall panel. Previous seller image card.

The archived people are generated illustrations of user situations, not real customers or endorsements. Current phone images are conceptual price illustrations, not screenshots of a live product or market feed. The whole originals are encoded as WebP quality 88 for delivery; card cropping is handled by CSS. Keep originals and delivery files separate when revising the artwork. Fonts and logos are separate assets, never baked into these photographs.
